PATCH 30.3.0I - SECOND SUPERVISED PAPER ORDER SUBMIT EXECUTION

Scope:
- Adds a paper-only, default-off execution boundary for the second supervised LSR-v2 paper order.
- Consumes the 30.3.0G second-order gate and 30.3.0H bounded multi-order readiness report.
- Requires explicit submit and execute confirmations before calling PaperBrokerAdapter.
- Submits at most one paper order and opens at most one paper position.
- Syncs paper_status.json from paper_state.json after a successful submit so local monitors can see the open position.

Safety:
- No live broker.
- No testnet broker.
- No exchange broker.
- No automatic close.
- No automatic re-entry.
- No scheduler startup.
- No third-order unlock.
